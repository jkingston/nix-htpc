The URL is on an upstream `main` branch, but Nix accepts it only when its
unpacked contents match the pinned hash. Updating upstream therefore requires
an explicit hash change and source review.

## Repository split

Mutable and reviewable source is stored locally:

- `addon.xml`, `LICENSE`, and upstream `README.md`;
- all `1080i`, `colors`, `language`, and `shortcuts` files;
- textual files from `extras`.

Large immutable payloads remain in the hash-pinned upstream fetch:

- compiled skin media (`media/Textures.xbt`);
- fonts;
- add-on icon and fanart;
- videos and raster artwork under `extras`.

`1080i/script-skinshortcuts-includes.xml` is intentionally not imported. It is
runtime-generated by Skin Shortcuts and must not replace the user's generated
copy during activation.

The upstream archive includes the GNU GPL version 2 license at
`src/LICENSE`. Binary fonts and artwork are retained exactly from that archive;
their individual provenance and redistribution terms have not been established
by this import and require a separate audit before redistribution.
